WebRTC Tic-Tac-Toe

Play a peer-to-peer game of tic-tac-toe using WebRTC data channels. No server is required beyond exchanging the connection text.

How to connect

  1. Player A clicks Create Offer and shares the generated text with Player B.
  2. Player B pastes the offer into the Remote SDP area and clicks Answer & Connect. They share the generated answer back.
  3. Player A pastes the answer and clicks Finalize Connection. Once the status turns green, the board becomes live.

Tip: send the text through chat, email, or QR. It expires when the page reloads.
