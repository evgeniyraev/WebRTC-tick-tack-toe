WebRTC Tic-Tac-Toe

A tiny wizard helps you host or join a peer-to-peer match using WebRTC data channels. No servers — just exchange the generated text.

Step 1

How do you want to play?

Invite someone and send them your offer, or paste an offer you already received.

Invite a friend

You will host the match, create an offer, send it to your friend, and wait for their answer.

  • You start first as X.
  • The game unlocks after your friend replies.

Accept an invite

Paste the host's offer, generate an answer, and send it back to lock in the connection.

  • You play as O.
  • You'll see the board once the channel opens.

Step 2

Connection setup

Choose a role to see detailed instructions.

Host flow: Create an offer, copy the generated text, send it to your friend, then paste their answer below and finalize the connection.
Guest flow: Paste the host offer, click “Answer & Connect”, share the generated answer back, and wait for the host to finalize.
